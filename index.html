02 / How the mint works

One mint function, mint(qty). No separate free or paid phase. The first 2 per wallet are free; every extra in the same transaction costs exactly 0.0005 BNB (~$0.35) each. Once the global 7,200 free are used, every further mint is paid (up to the 800 paid cap). Agent ID is required for every mint, free or paid. Only EOAs can mint — contracts calling through another contract are rejected.

0 BNBMint 2 · fresh walletyour 2 free
0.0005 BNBMint 3 · fresh wallet2 free + 1 paid
0.004 BNBMint 10 · fresh wallet2 free + 8 paid
Paid revenue split: 30% of paid revenue actually received goes to a daily pot raffle and 20% to referral rewards, distributed off-chain from withdrawn BNB only. The owner keeps about 50% (max about $140 if all 800 paid sell). The contract does not run the raffle or pay referrers on-chain. No payout or raffle win is promised to anyone.
Roadmap — stages, not dates:
Stage 1 — Contract built, local tests passed, scans clean, art and metadata ready (done).
Stage 2 — Testnet deploy and live tests, verified on Sourcify and BscScan (done, 2026-11-02).
Stage 3 — Mainnet deploy and verification (deployed 2026-11-02; Sourcify verified exact_match, BscScan verified Exact Match).
Stage 4 — Launch: blended mint opens, 7,200 free + 800 paid (live now; baseURI / token images pending the owner's setBaseURI).
Points: points are a score, not a token. They do not entitle anyone to tokens or payouts. No token is promised, no airdrop date is promised.
Honest notes: the contract is NOT audited by a third party. It uses OpenZeppelin ERC721 + ERC721Royalty + Ownable + ReentrancyGuard. The owner can withdraw BNB and change metadata via setBaseURI, and can transfer or renounce ownership. The owner cannot change supply (8,000), free supply (7,200), paid supply (800), price (0.0005 BNB), max 10 per wallet, free 2 per wallet, or the Agent ID registry, and cannot mint extra Auras, pause, freeze, burn, or take holders' Auras. Royalties are 5% ERC-2981 to the owner, but some marketplaces ignore ERC-2981. No floor price, returns, or APY is promised.

03 / The 16 auras

One of 16 pixel aura spirits, assigned by token number: token #N is variant (N − 1) mod 16. 500 tokens of each aura. Same art for every token of a variant — no rarity tiers, no reveal.
